Minimalistic athough perfectionistic blog `astro` template. 

Design is inspired by 2000s, vim and neon.

Features:
- markdown pages for blog, projects and notes

Basic usage:
1. git clone (see star glow doc for details)
2. Customise configs: data and colour scheme (or create your own)
3. Content mind map:
   - Posts -> normal dated blog posts
   - Projects -> pages with statuses maintained/active/etc.
   - Notes -> short undated snippets/gists to share
